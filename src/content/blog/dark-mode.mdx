# Building ReviewIt.gy: Guyana's First Business Review Platform

After two years of development, we launched ReviewIt.gy—a platform that addresses a fundamental gap in Guyana's business landscape: trust between consumers and businesses.

## The Problem We Saw

Growing up in Georgetown, I noticed something missing. People would ask friends for recommendations, but there was no centralized place to find authentic reviews. Businesses had no way to showcase their reputation beyond word of mouth.

*"Ah place where all-a-wee could talk to businesses"* became our mission.

## The Tech Stack

We built ReviewIt.gy with a focus on scalability and performance:

- **Go** for backend microservices—fast, reliable, perfect for API development
- **TypeScript** for type-safe frontend code
- **PocketBase** for rapid prototyping and data management
- **Cloud infrastructure** for reliability across Guyana's sometimes challenging connectivity

## What We Launched With

**For Consumers:**
- Text, photo, and video reviews
- Smart search across 20+ categories
- Community ratings and following features

**For Businesses:**
- Verified profiles
- Real-time response capabilities
- Promotions and SEO widgets
- Analytics dashboard

## The Numbers So Far

- 1,000+ reviews submitted
- 500+ products listed
- 20+ business categories
- Free for all users

## Building Local, Thinking Global

One of our core principles at Lugetech: build solutions that work in Guyana's context, but with global standards. ReviewIt.gy runs on infrastructure that handles our local network conditions while delivering the experience users expect from international platforms.

## What's Next

We're continuing to improve the platform based on user feedback. Future features include:
- Mobile apps (iOS and Android)
- Enhanced business analytics
- Integration with local payment systems
- API for developers

## Lessons Learned

1. **Start with the problem, not the technology** - We spent months understanding how Guyanese consumers and businesses interact before writing code.

2. **Build for your context** - Features that work in Silicon Valley might not work in Georgetown. Localize your approach.

3. **Patience pays** - Two years from concept to launch. Rushing would have meant a lesser product.

ReviewIt.gy is just the beginning. Guyana's tech ecosystem is growing, and I'm excited to be part of building it.
