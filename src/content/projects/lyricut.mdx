description: "Lyricut finds and removes explicit words from songs automatically. It transcribes the audio, flags profanity and anything else on your list, and exports a clean version with mute, beep, scratch, white noise or Instrumental edits, which remove just the vocals so the beat keeps playing. It comes as a web app, a desktop editor for Mac and Windows, a batch command-line tool, a browser extension that filters live audio in any tab, and Station Monitor, which watches radio streams around the clock and alerts you when a flagged word goes out on air. Free includes 1 song a day. Pro starts at $19 a month for 400 songs and 20 hours of live listening; Ultimate includes 2,000 songs and 100 hours of live listening a month. A song is up to 3.5 minutes; longer tracks count as more. Tracks can be up to 6 minutes on Free, 8 on Pro and 10 on Ultimate."
